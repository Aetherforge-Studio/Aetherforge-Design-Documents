# Story & Narrative

_The story the player experiences: its central conflict, major events, themes, and how its narrative unfolds through gameplay. Focus on what happens and how the player discovers it. Worldbuilding details belong in [[GDD-03 - World & Setting]], character profiles belong in [[GDD-05 - Characters]], and conversation mechanics belong in the [[SYS-14 - Dialogue]] system. Not every game needs a story; use this page when narrative is part of the intended experience._

The conflict may be external, internal, interpersonal, societal, or rooted in a mystery or unanswered question. Keep this concise; detailed events belong in the story outline._

_Example (Bramblewick, a sample game): A wandering herbalist settles in a forgotten woodland village and begins restoring its abandoned homes. As the community slowly returns, the herbalist uncovers clues about why the village was deserted and must reconcile the desire to preserve its past with the changes needed to give it a future._
